# Play Console — Native App Content Draft

## Ads
**No** — the current native app does not intentionally include an advertising SDK and is not designed around in-app advertising.

## App access
Core learning and guide content can be browsed without a student account. Login is used for profile, progress and account-related features. No special reviewer credential should be required to open core educational content.

## Target audience
Primary audience: Japanese-language students and learners, especially Bangla-speaking learners in Japan and abroad. The app is not specifically designed for children under 13.

## Content rating notes
Educational Japanese-language content, quizzes, Japan Daily News, Japan-life information, job-interview/CV guidance, AI Tutor, Halal Scanner and student utilities. No intended violence, sexual content, gambling or controlled-substance promotion.

AI Tutor responses are generated from user requests and should be treated as educational assistance. Halal ingredient screening is informational and is not presented as official certification unless a verified certificate source exists.

## Permissions / reviewer notes
- Camera: Halal Scanner barcode detection.
- Photos: optional CV photo selection.
- Notifications: optional local study reminder.
- No precise-location, contacts, SMS/call-log or microphone permission is intentionally required by the current native feature set.

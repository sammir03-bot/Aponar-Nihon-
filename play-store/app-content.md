# Play Console — App Content Draft

## Ads
**No** — the Android wrapper itself does not include an ad SDK and the current Aponar Nihon experience is not designed around in-app advertising.

## App access
Core learning content can be browsed without a student account. Login is used for profile/progress/activity features. No special reviewer credential should be required to open the core app.

## Target audience
Primary audience: students and adult learners studying Japanese, especially Bangla-speaking learners. The app is not specifically designed for children under 13.

## Content rating notes
Educational language-learning content, quizzes, Japan-life information, job-interview/CV guidance and student utilities. No intended violence, sexual content, gambling or controlled-substance promotion.

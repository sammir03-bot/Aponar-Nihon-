## Full description (English)
Aponar Nihon is a native Japanese-learning and Japan-life companion designed for Bangla-speaking learners in Japan and abroad.

Study JLPT N5, N4 and N3 with vocabulary, kanji, grammar, reading, quizzes and mock tests. Practice with AI Tutor, read Japan Daily News with optional furigana and Bangla explanations, use the Halal Scanner for product and ingredient information, create a Japanese CV PDF, and explore interview, SSW, e-book and Japan-life resources.

Registered learners can use the same Aponar Nihon account across the website and app and keep supported learning progress connected to their account.

- Notification permission is optional and used for local study reminders.

## Full description (English)
Aponar Nihon is a Japanese learning and student-life companion designed for Bangla-speaking learners in Japan and abroad.

Study JLPT N5, N4 and N3 with vocabulary, kanji, grammar, useful phrases, quizzes and mock tests. Practice interview preparation, explore Japan-life and SSW guides, read study e-books, and use practical tools including a Japanese CV Builder and a part-time time manager.

Registered learners can maintain a student profile and keep learning activity/progress connected to their account.

## Required provenance record

For each third-party asset that remains in the repository, maintainers should record, where applicable:

1. file/path;
2. title or description;
3. original author or rightsholder;
4. original source URL or publication;
5. applicable license, permission, quotation basis, or other authorization;
6. required attribution text;
7. date the permission/license was checked; and
8. a durable copy or reference to permission evidence when redistribution depends on individual permission.

## If rights cannot be verified

If redistribution rights cannot be verified, do not assume that public availability means the material is free to redistribute. Prefer linking to the lawful source, replacing the material with original or clearly licensed content, or excluding it from public/package releases until the rights status is resolved.
